    Subsection 1. Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic) Data Set

    Predict whether the cancer is benign or malignant.
    🔗

    Dataset.

    Download the archived ZIP dataset
    🔗

    Content.

    • ID number - not a feature
      🔗
    • target: diagnosis (M = malignant, B = benign)
      🔗
    • Each cell nucleus has the following 10 features:
      1. radius (mean distance from center to perimeter)
        🔗
      2. texture (standard deviation of gray-scale values)
        🔗
      3. perimeter
        🔗
      4. area
        🔗
      5. smoothness (local variation in radius lengths)
        🔗
      6. compactness (\(\text{perimeter}^2 / \text{area} − 1.0\))
        🔗
      7. concavity (severity of concave contour portions)
        🔗
      8. concave points (number of concave contour portions)
        🔗
      9. symmetry
        🔗
      10. fractal dimension ("coastline approximation" − 1)
        🔗
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗

    Goal.

    Train a model to predict whether the cancer is benign or malignant.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection 2. Default of Credit Card Clients Dataset

    Default Payments of Credit Card Clients in Taiwan from 2005.
    🔗

    Dataset.

    This dataset contains default payments, demographic factors, credit data, payment history, and bill statements from April through September 2005. Download the archived ZIP dataset.
    🔗
    🔗

    Content.

    There are 25 variables:
    ID
    🔗
    ID of each client.
    🔗
    LIMIT_BAL
    🔗
    Amount of given credit in NT dollars (including individual and family/supplementary credit).
    🔗
    SEX
    🔗
    Gender (1 = male, 2 = female).
    🔗
    EDUCATION
    🔗
    1 = graduate school, 2 = university, 3 = high school, 4 = others, 5 = unknown, 6 = unknown.
    🔗
    MARRIAGE
    🔗
    1 = married, 2 = single, 3 = others.
    🔗
    AGE
    🔗
    Age in years.
    🔗
    PAY_0, PAY_2, …, PAY_6
    🔗
    Repayment status from September through April 2005: −1 = pay duly; 1–8 = payment delay for that many months; 9 = delay of nine months and above.
    🔗
    BILL_AMT1–BILL_AMT6
    🔗
    Bill-statement amounts in NT dollars from September through April 2005.
    🔗
    PAY_AMT1–PAY_AMT6
    🔗
    Previous-payment amounts in NT dollars from September through April 2005.
    🔗
    default.payment.next.month
    🔗
    Default payment (1 = yes, 0 = no).
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Goal.

    Train a model to predict whether the client will have default payment next month.

    Subsection 6. League of Legends Diamond Ranked Games (10 min)

    Classify LoL ranked games outcomes from the first 10 minutes of data.
    🔗

    Dataset.

    League of Legends is a MOBA where blue and red teams face off across three lanes, a jungle, and five roles; the goal is to take down the enemy Nexus to win the game. Download the archived ZIP dataset.
    🔗
    The dataset contains first 10-minute statistics for approximately 10,000 ranked games (SOLO QUEUE) from a high ELO (Diamond I to Master). Players have roughly the same level. Each game is unique; The gameId can help you fetch more attributes from the Riot API. There are 19 features per team (38 total) collected after 10min in-game. This includes kills, deaths, gold, experience, level... It’s up to you to do some feature engineering to get more insights. The column blueWins is the target value (the value we are trying to predict). A value of 1 means the blue team has won. 0 otherwise.
    🔗
    🔗

    Glossary.

    Warding totem
    🔗
    An item that a player can put on the map to reveal the nearby area. Very useful for map/objectives control.
    🔗
    Minions
    🔗
    NPC that belong to both teams. They give gold when killed by players.
    🔗
    Jungle minions
    🔗
    NPC that belong to NO TEAM. They give gold and buffs when killed by players.
    🔗
    Elite monsters
    🔗
    Monsters with high hp/damage that give a massive bonus(gold/XP/stats) when killed by a team.
    🔗
    Dragons
    🔗
    Elite monster which give team bonus when killed. The 4th dragon killed by a team gives a massive stats bonus. The 5th dragon (Elder Dragon) offers a huge advantage to the team.
    🔗
    Herald
    🔗
    Elite monster which gives stats bonus when killed by the player. It helps to push a lane and destroys structures.
    🔗
    Towers
    🔗
    Structures you have to destroy to reach the enemy Nexus. They give gold.
    🔗
    Level
    🔗
    Champion level. Start at 1. Max is 18.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Goal.

    Train a model to predict whether the blue team will win.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection 8. Mushroom Classification

    Safe to eat or deadly poison?
    🔗

    Dataset.

    Although this dataset was originally contributed to the UCI Machine Learning repository nearly 30 years ago, mushroom hunting (otherwise known as "shrooming") is enjoying new peaks in popularity. Learn which features spell certain death and which are most palatable in this dataset of mushroom characteristics. And how certain can your model be? Download the archived ZIP dataset.
    🔗
    🔗

    Content.

    This UCI dataset describes hypothetical samples for 23 species of gilled mushrooms in the Agaricus and Lepiota family, from The Audubon Society Field Guide to North American Mushrooms (1981). Each species is identified as definitely edible, definitely poisonous, or of unknown edibility and not recommended. This latter class was combined with the poisonous one. The Guide clearly states that there is no simple rule for determining the edibility of a mushroom; no rule like "leaflets three, let it be" for Poisonous Oak and Ivy. Donated to UCI ML on April 27, 1987.

    🔗
